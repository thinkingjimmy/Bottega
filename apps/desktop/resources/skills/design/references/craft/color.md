# Color

- Define colors by role—surface, text, border, accent, success, warning, danger—not by component.
- Use at most two accent hues and keep semantic colors distinguishable from the accent.
- Verify contrast in every state, including disabled, selected, dark, and high-emphasis surfaces.
- Tint neutrals coherently toward the product hue; do not invert a light palette mechanically for dark mode.
- Test color-blind differentiation with shape, label, or icon support.
