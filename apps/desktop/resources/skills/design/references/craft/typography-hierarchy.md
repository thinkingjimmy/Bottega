# Interface typography hierarchy

- Use at most two families and usually four sizes per screen.
- Keep adjacent scale steps at least 1.2× apart or differentiate with weight and spacing.
- Use 14px as a practical minimum for non-reading body content.
- Give uppercase labels at least 0.06em tracking and reduce their size one step.
- Align changing numbers with tabular figures and right-align comparable columns.
- Avoid using muted color so aggressively that important supporting text loses contrast.
