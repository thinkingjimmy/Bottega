# Practical UX laws

- Reduce visible choice when choices are equivalent; reveal complexity near the decision that needs it.
- Make frequent targets larger and closer, especially at screen edges and on touch.
- Group related items through spacing before adding boxes or decoration.
- Keep system response visible within roughly 100ms; show progress for longer work.
- Match established platform expectations unless the new behavior produces a measurable benefit.
- Let people recognize options instead of memorizing modes, codes, or hidden gestures.
