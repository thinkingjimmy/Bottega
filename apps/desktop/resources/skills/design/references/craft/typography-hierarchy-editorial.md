# Editorial typography hierarchy

- Set Latin reading lines to roughly 45–75 characters and CJK lines to 15–35.
- Use 16px or larger body text with 1.4–1.6 Latin or 1.6–1.9 CJK line-height.
- Separate headline, deck, body, caption, and metadata by role, not arbitrary styling.
- Let paragraph and section rhythm carry structure before adding rules or cards.
- Keep emphasis scarce and preserve link, quote, list, code, and footnote distinctions.
