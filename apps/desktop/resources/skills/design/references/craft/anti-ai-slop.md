# Anti-AI-slop review

- Reject generic indigo, gratuitous gradients, default glass, emoji icons, and uniform card grids unless the brief supplies a reason.
- Do not fabricate metrics, testimonials, company facts, or decorative charts.
- Replace filler sections and copy with specific hierarchy or explicit placeholders.
- Choose one depth system, one radius family, and a deliberate type character.
- Make materially different directions differ in layout and information strategy, not tint.
- Treat the product lint as advisory evidence; human intent remains authoritative.
