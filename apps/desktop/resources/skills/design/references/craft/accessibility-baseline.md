# Accessibility baseline

- Preserve a logical heading and landmark outline; do not use visual size as semantics.
- Every control must be reachable and operable by keyboard with a visible focus ring.
- Give icon-only controls an accessible name and keep labels associated with inputs.
- Meet 4.5:1 body-text and 3:1 large-text/essential-control contrast.
- Keep interactive targets at least 44×44px and never encode state with color alone.
- Respect reduced motion and verify zoom/reflow without clipping essential content.
